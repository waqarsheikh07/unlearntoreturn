Application Received

Thank you for applying. This program is not for everyone — and that is by design. We handpick every single candidate. If your reasoning was compelling and your intent genuine, you will hear from us. If not, you will not.

What happens next

  1. We read every word. Every application is reviewed individually and candidates are handpicked.
  2. If your reasoning was specific and compelling, you will be informed via email. Check your inbox (and spam folder).
  3. Not everyone will be accepted. Generic or weak applications are rejected. This program is earned, not given.
